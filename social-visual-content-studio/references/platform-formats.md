# Platform and format adaptation

Confirm current platform specifications when exact dimensions, duration, or interface safe zones matter; platforms change. If verification is unavailable, label dimensions as proposed rather than official.

## Format choice

Choose a carousel for definitions, comparisons, lists, frameworks, evidence-rich explanations, or content meant to be saved. Choose video for demonstrations, transformations, sequential processes, strong performance, or time-based explanation. A combined plan must assign different roles to each format instead of turning still pages into a mechanical slideshow.

## Common starting points

Use these only as editable project defaults:

| Placement | Working format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait carousel | 4:5 or 3:4 | Optimize for mobile reading and platform cropping |
| Vertical short video | 9:16 | Keep critical text away from interface overlays |
| LinkedIn document carousel | portrait PDF pages | Favor professional density and strong hierarchy |
| Square feed asset | 1:1 | Use when cross-platform compatibility matters |

## Carousel planning

Use the fewest pages that tell the full story:

1. Cover: a concrete promise, tension, or question.
2. Context: why the audience should care.
3. Development: mechanism, evidence, comparison, or steps.
4. Boundary: limitation, cost, exception, or uncertainty.
5. Close: decision, recap, or useful next action.

Simple topics often need 4–6 pages; complex topics often need 7–10. Do not add pages solely to meet a template.

## Short-video planning

- First 1–3 seconds: establish one tension, result, or surprise.
- Early section: identify the viewer's problem with minimal context.
- Middle: let each shot explain one idea through action, change, or demonstration.
- End: give a repeatable conclusion, fit, boundary, or next step.

Write a shot table with timecode, purpose, visual, motion, voiceover, on-screen text, transition, sound, source, and safe-zone note. Captions should be concise, legible, and timed to speech.

## Accessibility

- Maintain readable contrast and minimum practical mobile type size.
- Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
- Add useful alt text to images.
- Caption spoken video and identify important non-speech audio when relevant.
- Avoid rapid flashing and excessive motion; disclose sensitive imagery when necessary.

# Copy and quality assurance

## Publish copy

Deliver:

1. One recommended title or hook.
2. Two alternatives with meaningfully different angles.
3. A platform-appropriate caption.
4. Relevant tags or search keywords without spam.
5. Alt text for every still asset.
6. For video: cover copy, voiceover, on-screen text, and captions.

Match tone, length, vocabulary, emoji use, calls to action, and disclosure to the chosen audience and platform. Avoid unsupported urgency, fear, certainty, superlatives, testimonials, or first-person experience.

## Asset-by-asset review

Inspect originals rather than thumbnails. Compare against the content brief, storyboard, exact-text list, and sources. Mark each asset `pass` or `revise` and state the most important fix.

Check:

- correct aspect ratio, export size, crop, and safe area
- spelling, language, names, numbers, dates, and capitalization
- text hierarchy, contrast, mobile legibility, and alt text
- accurate mechanisms, interfaces, data, and source boundaries
- brand consistency without repetitive layout
- coherent action, sequence, character, props, and visual causality
- absence of unwanted logos, watermarks, private data, artifacts, and extra text
- non-deceptive edits and any disclosure required for synthetic media

## Set-level review

Confirm that the order tells one coherent story; the cover promise is fulfilled; repeated facts and decorative pages are removed; visual and verbal tone agree; source disclosures are present; and originals plus revisions remain available.

For video, additionally verify hook timing, one main idea per shot, caption synchronization, audio balance, continuity, platform overlays, frame rate, and final duration.

Require revision for factual error, unreadable or incorrect text, misleading depiction, privacy exposure, broken story logic, severe identity drift, cropping, or inaccessible contrast. Small decorative differences may pass if they do not affect comprehension or brand integrity.
